repository. It does not grant any right to the project's name, brand or visual

explicitly so nobody has to guess.

- The name **Recipely**, and any confusingly similar name.

- The App Store and Google Play listings, their screenshots and copy.
- The domains `recipely.net` and its subdomains.

You may not use these to publish, distribute or market a build of this software,
whether modified or not. A fork must carry its own name, its own icon and its own
listing.

## What you may do

Everything the AGPL allows: read, run, modify, self-host and redistribute the
code, provided your version is released under the AGPL too and you make its
source available to anyone who uses it over a network. Attribution to this
repository is welcome but is not what this file is about.

## What is not here

This repository is the mobile and web client. The backend it talks to
(`recipely-backend` — Node, Express, Prisma, PostgreSQL) is a separate, private
repository, and the API keys, encryption key and service credentials the app
needs at runtime are not committed anywhere in this tree. A checkout of this
repository is therefore a codebase to learn from and build on, not a deployable
copy of the product.

## Questions

If you want to use the brand for something — a write-up, a talk, a fork you want
to discuss — open an issue and ask. The reservation exists to stop a look-alike
in the stores, not to stop people talking about the project.
