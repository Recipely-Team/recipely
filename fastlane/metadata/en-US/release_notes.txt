Fixes for the voice assistant, every one of them reported by people using it.

• The keyboard no longer covers the conversation. Typing to the assistant pushed both the chat and the box you type into underneath the keyboard — on tablets as well as phones.
• Typing to the assistant from the recipe list works. It used to fail every time, and tell you the request had not arrived.
• Asking it to change a draft changes that draft, instead of starting a new recipe over the top of the one you were writing.
• "Make it for eight people" now rescales the ingredients along with the serving count. It used to change the number and leave the quantities alone, which left the recipe disagreeing with itself.

Thanks for cooking with Recipely!
