Cook by talking, with your hands in the dough.

The voice assistant has been reworked from end to end in this release.

• You can interrupt it. Speak while it is talking and it stops to listen — no waiting for the answer to finish.
• It reads the recipe to you. Ask for the ingredients, the next step, the nutrition figures or how long something takes. Asking it to read no longer ticks the step off; reading and checking off are separate again.
• It drives search, filters and sorting by voice: show me Turkish food, only the easy ones, sort by newest. Cuisine, difficulty and sort choices are actually applied now.
• It saves and likes a recipe — and unliking really unlikes.
• It scrolls as much as you asked: a little further, back to the top, all the way down. Where it cannot scroll, it no longer claims it did.
• It does not report work it has not done. When something fails, it tells you.
• It starts on the first tap and warns you before your speaking time runs out.
• It will not open links that leave the app, such as the privacy policy and terms — it says plainly that it cannot.
• It knows every screen it can reach, including the featured recipes on the home page.

The assistant's language and voice are pinned too: it speaks the language you chose, without putting on an accent.

Thanks for cooking with Recipely!
