More places to bring a recipe in from, more control over when it goes public, and a clearer look at what is in it.

• Import a recipe from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube videos, from Pinterest pins, from any recipe website, or from photos and PDFs of a recipe's pages.
• The source link opens the original video and names the account it came from.
• Save a recipe privately first, and publish it from the recipe page when you are ready.
• A recipe imported from a website is someone else's work: before it can be published, give it your own photo and put the ingredients and steps in your own words.
• Nutrition is shown per 100 g or per serving, with a switch between the two.
• A new photo viewer: swipe through a recipe's photos, and each one is cropped on the dish. Cards show how many photos a recipe has.
• Import notifications say which platform a recipe came from, and a failed import no longer reads as a finished one.
• Translation fixes, including buttons and difficulty levels that still spoke English in other languages.
