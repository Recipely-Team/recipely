Cook with a timer running.

• Step timers. Start one from any step of a recipe and get an alarm when the time is up. One timer per recipe, and the running-timer bar can be tucked away when it covers something you need.
• Search now looks through the whole recipe catalogue rather than what was already on screen — and it finds Turkish names typed without their accents, so "sut" finds "Süt" and "pogaca" finds "Poğaça". The order you type the words in no longer matters either.
• Your likes stay liked after you close and reopen the app.
• Comments send on the first tap, and nutrition says when a recipe has no figures instead of quietly showing nothing.
• Drafts behave. Opening one and leaving without changing anything no longer re-saves it, and "leave without saving" actually removes it.
• Ingredient rows no longer split a word between the amount badge and the name.
• Signing out asks first, and continuing without an account no longer leaves the sign-in screen a back gesture away.
• A new app icon, and Turkish wording corrected throughout.

Editing a published recipe has been removed for now while we rework it. You can still create, delete and draft recipes.
