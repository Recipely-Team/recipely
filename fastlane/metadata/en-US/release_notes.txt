Fixes for things people reported while using the assistant, and one new way to find your recipes.

• The assistant no longer refuses to make a recipe because of a draft you cannot see. It kept asking what to do with a draft that was not on the screen, and there was no way out of the conversation.
• Ask for a new recipe while you are editing one, and it now asks what to do with the draft first — keep it or discard it — and makes the recipe you asked for either way.
• iOS: a developer message that could appear over the app on launch, more than once, is gone.
• Siri recognises the app's name more reliably, however you pronounce it.
• Recipes you have saved or created can now be found from your phone's own search, and open straight to the recipe.

Thanks for cooking with Recipely!
