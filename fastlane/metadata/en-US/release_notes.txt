The assistant can read the screen you are on.

• Ask it to read the page. A draft you are still writing, a recipe, your own list, your notifications, your settings — it reads out what is there. It no longer tells you to save or open something you are already looking at.
• It reads a draft's ingredients and steps, not only a published recipe's. A recipe it has just written for you can be read back before you publish it.
• "Save" in the recipe editor now means what the button means: it asks you to confirm, then publishes.
• When something goes wrong, it sends the report itself. Say "tell the developers" and it goes — with the error and the screen you were on attached. You no longer have to find the feedback form.
• It reads the comments under a recipe along with the rest of the page.
• "Refresh" works on the home feed, as it already did on your recipes and your notifications.
• When it cannot do something, it says so, instead of guessing where a button might be.

Also fixed: a crash on a developer screen that could be reached from inside the app.

Thanks for cooking with Recipely!
