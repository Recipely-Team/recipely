Recipely is your AI kitchen assistant — the fastest way from "that looked good" to dinner on the table.

A VOICE ASSISTANT THAT USES THE APP FOR YOU
Tap the chef and just talk. Ask it to find chicken recipes, add a vegetarian filter, open your saved recipes or create a lentil soup — it does it on screen while you watch, and answers out loud. Prefer typing? Write to it instead.

IMPORT FROM ANYWHERE
Share a cooking video from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, a Pinterest pin, any recipe website — or photograph a cookbook page or pick a PDF. Recipely writes it out as a proper recipe: ingredients, portions and ordered steps, with a link back to the original.

AI RECIPE GENERATION
Describe what you feel like cooking, or list the ingredients you already have, and the AI drafts the whole recipe for you. Then change it by chatting: make it vegetarian, double the portions, skip an ingredient.

SAVE PRIVATELY, PUBLISH WHEN READY
Every recipe is saved privately first. Publish it from the recipe page when you are ready.

NUTRITION PER 100 G OR PER SERVING
Calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre, with a switch between per 100 g and per serving.

STEP TIMERS WITH NOTIFICATIONS
Start a timer from any cooking step. It keeps running in the background and notifies you when the time is up.

YOUR OWN COOKBOOK
Saved, created and draft recipes stay organised in My Recipes.

THEMES AND 14 LANGUAGES
Four colour themes with full dark and light mode, in 14 languages.
