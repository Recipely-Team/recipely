Meet Recipely — the AI kitchen assistant that turns a food video, a craving or a half-empty fridge into a recipe you can actually cook.

🎙 A VOICE ASSISTANT THAT USES THE APP FOR YOU
Tap the chef and just talk. Ask it to find chicken recipes, add a vegetarian filter, open your saved recipes or create a lentil soup — it does it on screen while you watch, and answers out loud. Prefer typing? Write to it instead.

📲 IMPORT FROM ANYWHERE
Share a cooking video from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, a Pinterest pin, any recipe website — or photograph a cookbook page or pick a PDF. Recipely writes it out as a proper recipe: ingredients, portions and ordered steps, with a link back to the original and the account it came from.

🤖 AI RECIPE GENERATION
Type what you want to cook, or list what you have at home. The AI writes a recipe made for you, with clear quantities and instructions — then change it by chatting: vegetarian, for six, no oven.

🔒 SAVE PRIVATELY, PUBLISH WHEN READY
Every recipe is saved privately first. Publish it from the recipe page when it is ready for everyone.

📊 NUTRITION PER 100 G OR PER SERVING
Calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre, with a switch between per 100 g and per serving, and each macro's share of your day.

⏱ STEP TIMERS THAT KEEP RUNNING
Start a timer inside any step. It keeps running in the background and notifies you when it's done.

🔖 YOUR OWN COOKBOOK
Saved, created and draft recipes stay sorted in My Recipes.

🎨 THEMES AND 14 LANGUAGES
Four colour themes in dark and light mode, in 14 languages.
